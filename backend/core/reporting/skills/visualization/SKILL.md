# Visualization Skill

Chart selection and quality rules for mining reports.

## Selection

- Temporal trends: line chart, one line per entity, max 6 series.
- Entity comparison in one period: grouped bars.
- Composition in one period: shares bars (avoid pie over 5 categories).
- Two-series comparison (production vs offtake): grouped bars with legend.

## Rules

- Title states the finding, not the topic ("Production rose 8.4%").
- Axes always labeled with units; fiscal labels on x.
- No 3D effects, no dual axes unless units are stated on both.
- Every chart has a source line naming the origin table or fact set.
