# Government Reporting Skill

Language and structure for technical reports to authority.

## Language

- Plain sentences, present facts before interpretation.
- Fiscal years as FY labels; quantities with units every time.
- No adjectives without numbers ("significant" is banned without a value).
- Recommendations are conditional on evidence ("subject to verification of…").

## Structure

- Cover data: title, scope, date, source count.
- Findings before recommendations, always.
- Verification notes disclose every unresolved conflict.
